Water, sunlight and wind meet within a single painterly space. Their distinct rhythms come together through colour, texture and movement, creating an image of mutual support. Hydropower appears as a unifying force, helping to balance the variability of natural flows. “Union of the Elements” reveals the beauty of an energy system through the relationships between its parts: each retains its own character while contributing to a shared equilibrium. It reflects on humanity's ability to listen to nature and bring harmony to its many voices.
