The sun unfolds above the geometry of solar panels as a source of constant movement and renewal. Warm currents of colour meet the precise rhythm of technological forms, linking a distant celestial body with life on Earth. Here, solar energy becomes tangible: it passes across the canvas, filling it with light and warmth. The painting speaks of humanity's desire to draw closer to a natural source of power, to learn to receive its gifts and transform them into the foundation of the future.
