The solar disc unites the two panels of the diptych, turning them into a shared horizon. Its radiance passes through the geometry of an energy landscape, connecting natural light with human-made forms. Renewable energy appears as an extension of the Earth's rhythms — the sun's daily journey and the free movement of air. “Rising Energy” conveys a sense of anticipation and beginning: the world still holds deep shadows, yet it is already filling with light. This ascent carries hope for a future that humanity creates while maintaining its bond with nature.
