Light geometric cells form an ordered structure enlivened by accents of colour. Metal, glass and wood lend material depth to the image, allowing the atomic theme to emerge through the interplay of light and surface. The matrix suggests a reactor: a complex whole in which every element matters. The work reveals the expressive quality of engineering order and the hidden power within it. Its restrained geometry recalls the precision, care and responsibility required of humanity in working with nuclear energy.
