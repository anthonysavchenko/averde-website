A cool palette and shimmering texture evoke a landscape gripped by freezing rain. The structures of power transmission lines maintain their rhythm amid the freezing currents, becoming a symbol of resilience in the face of the elements. Their stillness holds tension: electricity must continue its journey even when nature itself tests the system's strength. The painting is also dedicated to the people whose work remains outside the frame. Their presence is felt in the connection that is preserved, and in the ability to restore movement where cold threatens to bring it to a halt.
