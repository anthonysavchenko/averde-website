The circular format of the canvas heightens the sense of continuous movement. Interwoven lines and currents of colour form an image of energy travelling through countless connections. Here the electrical grid acquires painterly substance: its invisible work becomes a visible circulation of light. “Energy Rings” draws attention to the integrity of a vast system connecting distant places and human lives. Every line contributes to the overall movement, and behind this intricate pattern one senses the daily labour of those who keep it running without interruption.
