The turbine appears as a majestic creature, combining the strength of a beast with the precision of a machine. Its metallic rhythms evoke a mane, while the depth of the dark tones emphasises its inner power. “King of Energy” embodies concentrated, directed force — energy that humanity has learned to harness and transform into motion. The image alludes to gas-fired power generation and the work of machine builders. Behind this monumental technological portrait lie the knowledge and determination of the people who make energy a foundation of everyday life.
