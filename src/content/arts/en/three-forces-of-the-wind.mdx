The three panels reveal different states of a single natural force. Figures, blades and air currents flow from one image to the next, linking human movement with the rotation of wind turbines. Wind becomes a visual language through which nature and technology converse. The rhythm of the triptych allows the viewer to experience this encounter in sequence and then perceive it as a whole. The monumentality of engineering structures meets the lightness of dance, transforming wind power into an image of a living, constantly changing force.
