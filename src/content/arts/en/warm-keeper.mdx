The majestic lion, in the form of which natural power connects with industrial forms, embodies the force, reliability, and creative energy of the thermal network complex.

Fiery streams of red, orange, and gold, piercing its mane and metallic structures, symbolize the continuous flow of heat — from its production sources through branched thermal networks to cities, enterprises, and homes.

Mechanical elements become part of a living organism, embodying the unity of natural forces and engineering thought.

The lion serves as a guardian of vital energy — a symbol of responsibility, sustainability, and the invisible labor of people who ensure that heat reaches every home.

The work is dedicated to thermal energy infrastructure — an industry that provides comfort, safety, and quality of life for millions of people.
