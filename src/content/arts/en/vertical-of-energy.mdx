The upward-reaching form evokes a sprout, a flame and a natural gesture aspiring towards the sky. Darhu wood, fibreglass and gold bring together an organic foundation and materials transformed by human hands. The sculpture presents energy as the movement of growth — from earth towards light, from a hidden impulse to visible form. Its verticality connects renewable energy with the life force of nature. In this image, the development of energy becomes part of a reflection on a future in which human creativity preserves space for life.
